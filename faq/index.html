SCHEDULING

How fast can you get a post in the ground?

Standard requests are completed within 3 business days of your selected start date. Need it sooner? Choose the next-day option in the order form: requests submitted between 5:00 AM and 5:00 PM CT are installed the next business day. Requests outside that window are installed the following business day.

Do you offer rush installs?

Yes. Rush availability depends on the schedule and your listing's location. Pick the next-day option in the order form, or mention your deadline in the notes, and we'll confirm availability.

How do I know my request is actually scheduled?

You'll receive a confirmation within 24 hours once your request is reviewed. That confirmation is your "it's scheduled" signal. Nothing yet? Call us at (817) 760-8288.

What should I put in the order notes?

Anything we need to get it right the first time: gate codes, lot flags, lockbox instructions, builder or HOA restrictions, and where you want panels and riders returned. More detail up front means fewer callbacks and no delays.

PLACEMENT & COVERAGE

Where on the property will you place the post?

We place for maximum visibility and common-sense curb appeal. If your HOA or city requires a specific spot, note it in the order and we'll follow it.

What areas do you serve?

We focus on Tarrant County: Fort Worth, Arlington, Mansfield, Keller, Southlake, Grapevine, North Richland Hills, Colleyville, Bedford, Euless, Hurst, and nearby. Service expands to the full DFW Metroplex in 2026. See the coverage map.

PRICING & POSTS

How much does an install cost?

$65 for our TX Posts wood post with panel and rider hanging included, $65 to install your PVC or aluminum post, $55 for your metal frame, and $60 for custom posts. Every option includes free removal when the listing sells. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Can you install a post or frame I already own?

Yes. We install your PVC or aluminum post for $65, your metal frame for $55 (we can store the frame between listings too), and custom or specialty posts for $60.

What add-ons can I get?

Solar light ($15), brochure box ($15), extra riders ($10 each, first rider included), plus lockbox and Supra mounting. Add them right in the order form at install time, no separate trip needed. See the add-ons section on the pricing page.

REMOVAL & AFTER CLOSING

My listing sold. How do I get the post removed?

Congrats! Submit a removal request and we'll pull the post within 3 business days of your selected date, with a next-day option if you need it gone fast. Removal is free for every post type when the listing sells.

What happens to my panels and riders after removal?

Your call. We can keep them in TX POSTS storage for free, return them to your brokerage, or drop them at an address you give us. Riders can also be left on the front porch at pickup.

Do you store signs between listings?

Yes, free storage is available. Mention it in your order and we'll hold your panels, riders, or metal frame until your next listing.

A storm knocked my post crooked. Now what?

Submit a request through the order form and pick the repair option. Repairs and straightening are free on TX Posts wood posts. For posts you own: $45 for PVC or aluminum, $35 for metal frames, $40 for custom posts.
